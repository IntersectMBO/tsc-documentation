# Commissioned Research & Benchmarking

The Technical Steering Committee (TSC) has allocated up to **160,000 ada** from its 2026 budget to the Parameter Committee (PC) for commissioning independent benchmarking and research on Cardano protocol parameters. The studies give the PC an evidence base for its parameter recommendations that is gathered independently of node vendors and implementation teams.

## RFP process

Each study is commissioned through a public Request for Proposals (RFP):

1. **Scope** — The PC identifies a parameter question that needs independent evidence (for example, how far `utxoCostPerByte` can safely be reduced) and publishes an RFP describing the question and expected deliverables.
2. **Publish** — The RFP is shared on the Cardano Forum, X, and other community channels. Each RFP has its own submission form and deadline.
3. **Submit** — Individuals or teams submit a short proposal via the form, typically covering:
   * research approach and methodology
   * relevant experience or qualifications
   * estimated timeline
   * a fixed-price quotation in ada
4. **Select** — The PC evaluates the proposals and selects who to commission.
5. **Deliver** — Part of the fixed price is paid when the work is commissioned; the remainder is paid on completion and acceptance of the work by the PC. The proposer presents their findings to the PC and answers technical questions from the PC and the community.
6. **Publish** — Final reports are published publicly.
