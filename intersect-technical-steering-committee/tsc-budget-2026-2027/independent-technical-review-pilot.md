# Independent Technical Review Pilot

### Purpose

DReps are asked to fund complex technical work with little independent advice on whether it is sound. This pilot provides short, independent technical reviews of core infrastructure treasury proposals, published before the vote, so DReps can see what experienced engineers think of a proposal's feasibility, cost and risks.

2026 is the set-up year: building the review framework and the reviewer pool, so that the pilot is ready to review proposals in the 2027 budget cycle.

### What gets reviewed

**Core infrastructure** means any proposal intended to directly affect the operation of the blockchain — including new features, maintenance, security or operations — or tooling that is essential for users to access the blockchain. This includes nodes, chain indexers, software and hardware wallets, block explorers, SDKs, and bridges.

Proposers request a review; the WP3 lead confirms eligibility. Reviews prioritise **open-source** offerings. If there are more requests than capacity, open-source proposals come first, then proposals are ordered by how soon their governance vote is.

### How a review works

1. **Request and eligibility check.**
2. **Conflict-of-interest check.** TSC members and representatives of affiliated organisations recuse from anything linked to them. Recusals are recorded and published.
3. **Assignment** of about three independent reviewers.
4. **Review** against the published review framework, each reviewer writing separately.
5. **Factual check.** The proposer has \[48 hours] to point out factual errors. Reviewers' judgements are not changed.
6. **Publication** of all reviews in full, before the governance voting deadline.

Reviews assess the proposal; they do not tell DReps how to vote.

### Reviewers

Recruited openly against \[published criteria]. The pool's membership and affiliations are published, and no single organisation holds a majority of reviewing capacity. Reviewer assignments are made by the WP3 lead.

The TSC Chair is not part of the reviewer pool; the Chair's role is limited to oversight of the process.
