# Parameter Committee Stipend

### Purpose

The Parameter Committee gives independent technical advice on Cardano's protocol parameters: proposed parameter changes, the guardrails that bound them, and the initial settings for new parameters introduced at hard forks. Its recommendations inform DReps, SPOs and the Constitutional Committee before they vote. This programme funds committee members' time, and commissions independent benchmarking studies to give the committee evidence that does not depend on any one node implementation.

### Scope for this period

* Reviewing proposed parameter changes and publishing recommendations, including changes to Plutus execution limits, and stake pool settings as applicable
* Guardrails and initial settings for the new Dijkstra parameters (around 100 for Leios and 50 for Peras)
* Cost model settings for Plutus v4
* Up to four independent benchmarking studies on questions where the committee needs evidence, e.g. Leios throughput limits, fee and reference-script pricing, block and transaction size limits

Compensation is fixed in ADA and does not track the USD price.

### The role

Funded members attend committee meetings, review the parameter proposals and guardrails as necessary, and contribute to its published recommendations. A short monthly note on what they've done is enough evidence for payment.

### Who can take part

Members are selected against published criteria: relevant expertise in protocol parameters, network performance, economics or ledger design, and capacity to commit to regular meetings. **Independence from node vendors is a published, checked criterion**, because the committee's advice and the benchmarking studies must not depend on any one implementation.

Existing committee members move directly into the funded roles; open recruitment fills any remaining seats up to the cap. Seats are non-exclusive: there is no forced rotation, but any vacancy is filled through open recruitment, and members are reconfirmed at the interim review.

### Benchmarking studies

Studies are separate, deliverable-based engagements, not part of the monthly stipend.

* **Scoping:** the committee agrees a study plan; each study gets a short statement of work (question, method, deadline, acceptance criteria, cap).
* **Selection:** open call \[link to RFP], with conflict-of-interest disclosure from bidders. Node vendors are not eligible to run studies.
* **Payment:** on delivery and acceptance against the criteria.
* **Publication:** results published in full, with the committee's recommendation.

### Conflict of interest

Members and study authors disclose relevant affiliations on appointment and refresh the disclosure at the interim review, and recuse themselves where they have a conflict.
