# Hard Fork Working Group Stipend

### Purpose

The Hard Fork Working Group coordinates ecosystem readiness for Cardano hard forks — making sure node implementations, wallets, tooling, exchanges, SPOs and governance participants are ready before a hard fork is enacted, and that risks and missing dependencies surface early. This programme funds that coordination formally for the 12-month budget period.

### Scope for this period

The focus is the **Protocol versions 12 and 13 (Dijkstra) hard forks**, which bring Linear Leios (CIP-164), nested transactions (CIP-118), Peras (CIP-140), fair min fees (CIP-23), pledge-leverage rewards (CIP-50), Plutus V4 and other ledger changes, and — for the first time — constitutional changes alongside technical ones. See the [Dijkstra upgrade overview](https://cardanoupgrades.docs.intersectmbo.org/dijkstra-era-upgrade/dijkstra-upgrade-overview) for the full list. The working group covers:

* Tracking ecosystem readiness against an agreed readiness framework
* Coordinating dependency and risk tracking across teams, including technical, ecosystem and security concerns
* Outreach to affected groups (SPOs, wallets \[including hardware wallets], exchanges, tooling, DReps, dApps, Intersect members, and the general Cardano community)
* Liaison with the Parameter Committee and Civics Committee on new guardrails and initial parameter settings, and with the constitutional amendment process
* Liaison with Intersect's Security Council on security-related issues
* Post-fork retrospectives
* Coordination with Intersect and developer teams (including node diversity, quality assurance, and performance evaluation)

### Budget and limits

| Item         | Detail                                                         |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Budget       | 144,000 ADA (≈ $36,000 at the proposal's $0.25 reference rate) |
| Seats        | Up to 6 funded experts                                         |
| Compensation | 2,000 ADA per expert per month                                 |
| Settlement   | ADA                                                            |

Compensation is fixed in ADA and does not track the USD price.

### The role

Each funded expert takes responsibility for one or more readiness areas (for example SPOs, wallets, exchanges, tooling, alternative nodes, or governance), keeps its status current, and raises problems early. A short monthly note on what they've done is enough evidence for payment.

### Who can take part

Funded experts are selected against published criteria: relevant hands-on experience; prior hard fork involvement; capacity to commit to regular meetings.

Existing working group members move directly into the funded roles so that Dijkstra preparation is not interrupted; open recruitment fills any remaining seats up to the cap. Seats are non-exclusive: there is no forced rotation, but any vacancy is filled through open recruitment, and members are reconfirmed at the interim review. Anyone can still take part in the working group unfunded.
