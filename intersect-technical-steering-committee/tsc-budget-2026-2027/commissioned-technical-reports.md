# Commissioned Technical Reports

### Purpose

The Technical Steering Committee (TSC) commissions independent technical reports on questions that matter to Cardano's future, written so that DReps and the wider community — not just engineers — can understand the issues before they vote or decide.

### Budget and limits

| Item       | Detail                               |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Budget     | $40,000 for the year                 |
| Reports    | Up to 4, at up to $10,000 each       |
| Settlement | USD, paid on delivery and acceptance |

### How it works

* **Topics** are chosen by the WP1 lead, roughly one a quarter, based on what's live in governance and community discussion.
* **Authors** are independent experts, chosen through an open call or direct invitation, and must disclose any conflict of interest.
* **Payment** is made on delivery and acceptance against the report's scope.
* **Publication:** every report is reviewed by the WP1 lead and published here in full, crediting the author and reviewer, ahead of any governance vote it is meant to inform.
