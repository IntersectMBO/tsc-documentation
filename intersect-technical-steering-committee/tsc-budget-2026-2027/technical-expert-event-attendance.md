# Technical Expert Event Attendance

### Purpose

This program pays for technical experts to represent Cardano at major events, where they explain Cardano's technology — Leios, Peras, the Dijkstra upgrade, governance and more — to the wider community and to DReps. The aim is clear, informed technical communication, not general attendance.

### Budget and limits

| Item       | Detail                                     |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Budget     | $24,000 for the year                       |
| Experts    | Around 12 trips (≈ $2,000 each on average) |
| Covers     | Economy flights and accommodation          |
| Settlement | USD, reimbursed against receipts           |

### Which events

We keep a short list of qualifying events, reviewed every quarter. It is not limited to the events named in the original budget proposal.

**Current shortlist:** RareEvo, Cardano Africa Tech Summit, European Blockchain Convention, CV Summit Zurich, Node Diversity Workshops

You can also propose an event that isn't on the list, if it fits the purpose above.

### Who can apply

Technical experts who can represent Cardano's technology credibly. Priority goes to people with a **confirmed speaking or panel role**. For a Cardano presence at an externally run event, confirm your slot with the organizer before applying.

### How to apply

Apply as early as you can — flights and hotels get more expensive closer to the event. Tell us:

* Your name and the event
* Your role there (speaking, panel, or attending)
* Estimated flight and accommodation cost
* How your attendance helps explain Cardano's technology to the community

Apply via: [TSC Events/Travel Request](https://forms.clickup.com/9015279944/f/8cnmga8-54195/F567LG5DR0SK8AKOLJ)

### How decisions are made

The WP1 lead approves applications against the remaining budget, prioritizing confirmed speaking roles. Applicants disclose any relevant affiliations; anyone with a conflict does not take part in the decision.

### After the event

* Costs are reimbursed against receipts
* A short note on what you presented and what came out of it, within \[two weeks] of the event
* The list of funded attendees is published

### Reporting

* **Interim review:** end of February 2027 (Week 26)
* **Year-end review:** end of August 2027

Both are published on this GitBook.
